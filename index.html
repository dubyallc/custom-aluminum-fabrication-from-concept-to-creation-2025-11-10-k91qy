About Duby LLC

Founded in 2010, Duby LLC emerged from a vision to revolutionize the aluminum fabrication industry. Our founders, experienced engineers with a passion for precision manufacturing, recognized the need for a partner who could deliver both innovation and reliability. Starting as a small workshop with just a handful of dedicated craftspeople, we've grown into a full-service fabrication facility while maintaining the same commitment to excellence and personalized service that defined our beginning. Today, we serve hundreds of clients across diverse industries, from aerospace and automotive to consumer electronics and architectural applications.

At Duby LLC, we believe that exceptional results come from combining cutting-edge technology with human expertise and unwavering commitment to our clients. Our mission is to be the most trusted partner for custom aluminum fabrication, delivering solutions that exceed expectations and drive our clients' success. We're committed to continuous improvement, sustainable practices, and fostering a culture of innovation where every team member contributes to our collective excellence. We invest heavily in training, equipment, and processes to ensure we remain at the forefront of our industry, ready to tackle the most challenging and complex fabrication projects.
